Account and data controls

Delete your VocaMingo account

You can permanently delete your VocaMingo account and associated cloud data from inside the Android app or request deletion here if you no longer have access to it.

✓

Your account will not be deleted without verification

We verify account ownership before completing an external request. Never send us a password, sign-in link, verification code, or other sensitive credential.

Fastest option

Delete from the Android app

If VocaMingo is still installed and you can access the account:

  1. Open VocaMingo and go to Settings.
  2. Select Delete Account & Data.
  3. Review the information shown by the app.
  4. For an email-protected account, select Send link, open the verification email on that device, and tap the secure link.
  5. Return to VocaMingo and confirm Delete permanently.

An anonymous profile without a connected email can be deleted directly after the app's confirmation step and does not require an email link.

Check Spam or Junk The verification email may take a minute to arrive. Check your Spam or Junk folder if you do not see it in your inbox.
No access to the app

Request deletion by email

Send the request from the email address connected to your VocaMingo account whenever possible. This helps us verify ownership and locate the correct account. If the profile was anonymous, include your platform, profile name, approximate last-use date, and any non-sensitive support details you still have.

Request account deletion

This button opens your email app. You can also email vocamingo@gmail.com with the subject “VocaMingo Account Deletion Request.” You do not need to reinstall the app.

We may ask for additional non-sensitive information when reasonably necessary to verify ownership. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Data we delete

  • Your Firebase Authentication identity and connected email, if any;
  • Your private profile, built-in avatar selection, and cloud progress;
  • Coins, stars, scores, completed game levels, and synced game statistics;
  • Synced learning and lesson progress; and
  • Your public leaderboard profile and associated ranking data.

Local and retained information

Data stored only on your device cannot be removed remotely. Use Reset Progress or uninstall VocaMingo to remove local app data from that device.

We may retain deletion-request correspondence when reasonably necessary to document the request, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Independent providers may retain data under their own policies.

Need help?

Read the VocaMingo Privacy Policy or visit VocaMingo Support. For privacy questions, email vocamingo@gmail.com.
