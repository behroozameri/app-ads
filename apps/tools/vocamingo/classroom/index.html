Privacy by clear boundaries

Useful progress stays visible. Private work stays private.

The teacher report is intentionally limited to the information needed to understand learning activity.

A teacher can see

  • Recognizable classroom name, built-in avatar, and platform
  • Score, completed levels, stars, and average game time
  • Highest level, completion count, and average time per game
  • Completed Real-Life English lessons and totals
  • Practiced Speaking parts and totals
  • Leitner saved-word and due-word counts

A teacher cannot see

  • The learner's email address
  • Speaking recordings or transcripts
  • Typed guesses or puzzle answers
  • Vocabulary search text
  • Custom Real-Life English answers
  • Private account credentials or sign-in links

Availability and requirements

Teacher Access and Classroom are optional features that may be opened gradually or paused through VocaMingo's app configuration. A linked, verified email and a recognizable classroom name are required. Teacher applications are reviewed, and current class or membership limits are shown inside the app.

Common questions

Before you create or join a class.

Do I need an email to use VocaMingo?

No. General learning and game features can begin with an anonymous profile. A verified email is required only for account protection, Teacher Access, and Classroom participation.

Does entering a class code join me immediately?

No. VocaMingo first shows the class and teacher. After you send a request, the teacher must approve it before membership becomes active and progress becomes visible.

Can a teacher listen to my Speaking Practice recordings?

No. Saved audio and transcripts stay in the app's private local storage and are not included in the classroom report.

Can I belong to more than one class?

Yes, subject to the limits currently configured in the app. Approved teachers can also manage more than one class.

What happens when I leave a class?

The teacher immediately loses access to your progress report. An inactive membership status and related timestamps may remain for operational integrity until deletion under the retention process.

How do I remove all Classroom data?

Use Settings → Delete Account & Data, or follow the external deletion instructions if you no longer have the app. Account deletion includes Teacher Access, classes, and membership records, subject to limited security and legal exceptions.
