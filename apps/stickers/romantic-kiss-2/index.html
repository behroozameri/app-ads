Before you download

A few things to know

Are all packs available without watching an ad?

The apps contain advertising. Selected packs require a completed rewarded ad to unlock access. Unlocking a pack does not download it or add it to WhatsApp; choose those actions separately. Rewarded offers depend on availability and your consent settings.

Can I use the app offline?

Bundled artwork and completed pack downloads can be browsed offline. Discovering and downloading new packs, loading ads, and opening external services need an internet connection. Access requirements still apply to locked content.

Do I need an account or a messaging app?

No app account or sign-in is needed to browse. WhatsApp is required for WhatsApp export. On iOS, you can also copy images or use the standard share sheet; supported paste destinations may vary.
