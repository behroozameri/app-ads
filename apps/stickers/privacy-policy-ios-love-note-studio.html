Privacy Policy

Love Note Studio

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the iOS app Love Note Studio, version 2.0 and later, published by BPZone.

Love Note Studio is a romantic greeting and sticker creator. You can browse bundled artwork, choose message ideas, create layered designs in Note Studio, save favorites, copy or share finished images, and optionally export supported sticker packs to WhatsApp or Telegram.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS version of Love Note Studio. It does not apply to third-party websites, apps, advertising networks, or services that we do not control.

The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, make an in-app purchase, or install another app to use its core features.

2. Information You Provide Directly

We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, payment information, personal photos, camera access, microphone access, or precise location.

Text you type into the greeting editor is processed locally on your device to render the image preview and final exported image. We do not send that typed greeting text to our own servers.

3. Local On-Device Data

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device so the app can function properly:

  • Favorite artwork selections
  • Saved Note Studio drafts, text layers, layout choices, and editing settings
  • Previously shown featured artwork and message ideas, used to reduce repetition
  • Unlocked sticker-pack status and advertising frequency or cooldown information
  • Notification registration information, including the Firebase registration token
  • Basic app preferences, permission status, and review-prompt state
  • Temporary rendered image files when preparing content for sharing

Most of this data remains on your device. A notification registration token is also processed by Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging so notifications can be delivered when you allow them. You can remove local app data by deleting the app.

4. How Core App Features Work

Love Note Studio includes bundled sticker-style artwork and message templates. The greeting editor combines selected artwork, typed text, decorations, layers, layout, background, borders, filters, font, color, and other style choices locally on your device.

When you choose to copy or share a greeting, the app uses standard iOS features such as the pasteboard and share sheet. The destination you select in the share sheet may process the shared image according to that destination app's own privacy policy.

The optional “Add to WhatsApp” feature prepares the selected sticker pack locally and hands it to WhatsApp through its supported iOS integration. Pasteboard data used for this handoff is local to the device and configured to expire after a short period. The optional “Add to Telegram” feature opens an official HTTPS t.me sticker-pack link. If either app is unavailable, Love Note Studio's core browsing, editing, favorites, copy, and sharing features remain available.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party SDKs to support advertising, consent choices, analytics, remote configuration, and push notifications. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Google AdMob

Love Note Studio uses Google AdMob to display banner, native, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads may be offered as an optional way to unlock selected sticker collections. No payment is required, and ads are not required to use the app's core editor, available bundled artwork, favorites, copy, or share features.

Depending on your device settings, region, and consent choices, AdMob may process data such as:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version, language, and network details
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address
  • Advertising identifiers where available and permitted by platform rules and user choices
  • Ad interaction data, such as ad requests, impressions, clicks, and fraud-prevention signals
  • Diagnostics, crash, and performance information related to ad delivery

Google may use this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance, limit fraud and abuse, improve services, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

Learn more from the Google Privacy Policy, Google Advertising Policy, and Google partner sites and apps explanation.

Google User Messaging Platform

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may display a consent message and provide privacy choices where required. It may process consent status, approximate location derived from network information, device or app information, product interaction, and performance information to determine and operate the appropriate consent experience. When required, privacy choices can be reopened from the app's Info screen.

Firebase Analytics

The app may use Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and improve the product. Firebase Analytics events may include app opens, screen or feature usage, pack opens, and studio actions such as opening the greeting editor.

Firebase may process app instance identifiers, device and app information, and event metadata for analytics. We do not use Firebase Analytics to collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, personal photos, or the custom text you type into the greeting editor.

Learn more from Firebase Privacy and Security.

Firebase Remote Config

Firebase Remote Config lets us configure supported app features and advertising behavior without requiring a new app download. It may process information such as country code, language, time zone, operating-system version, app version, Firebase app identifier, bundle identifier, SDK version, and related diagnostics. Remote Config does not contain the custom greeting text you enter in Note Studio.

Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification Service

If you allow notifications, Apple Push Notification service (APNs) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) process a device-specific notification token and app-installation identifier to route notifications to your device. Firebase may also process device model, language, time zone, operating-system version, app identifier, app version, delivery information, and notification interaction events. Notification permission is optional and can be changed at any time in iOS Settings. Declining notifications does not limit the app's core features.

Learn more from Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Tracking, Ads, and Privacy Choices

Google Mobile Ads may use a device identifier for advertising and measurement, and certain identifiers may be used for tracking as Apple defines that term where permitted by platform rules, applicable law, device settings, and consent choices. The app's App Store privacy disclosure therefore identifies Device ID as data that may be used for tracking.

The current version does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier through an App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can manage applicable advertising choices through iOS Settings. Where required, Google UMP displays a consent message and makes a Privacy Choices control available from the app's Info screen.

Access to Love Note Studio's core features does not depend on accepting personalized ads or consenting to optional advertising purposes.

8. App Permissions

The app does not request access to Contacts, Photos, Camera, Microphone, Bluetooth, precise Location, Health, HomeKit, or Calendars. Copying and sharing use standard iOS system features.

The app may request permission to send alerts, sounds, and badge notifications. Notification permission is optional. You can change it at any time under iOS Settings > Notifications > Love Note Studio.

9. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers only as needed to operate, analyze, and monetize the app. These service providers include Google AdMob, Google UMP, Firebase, and Apple Push Notification service. Advertising partners may process device, ad, usage, and diagnostic information as described above. We do not sell your name, email address, phone number, contacts, messages, or personal photos for money, and the app does not collect those items from you.

If you deliberately use WhatsApp, Telegram, or another destination in the iOS share sheet, the selected content or sticker-pack link is provided to that service at your direction and is then handled under that service's own terms and privacy policy.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Local favorites, Note Studio drafts, settings, unlock status, notification token copy, and related preferences remain on your device until removed through available app behavior, reset by the operating system, or deleted with the app. Depending on your Apple device settings, local app data may be included in an Apple device or iCloud backup.

Temporary exported image files may be created when preparing content for sharing and are stored in the app's temporary storage. iOS or the app may clear temporary files over time.

You can stop future notifications by disabling notification permission in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its local registration token copy; Firebase and Apple manage server-side notification tokens and expiration according to their systems and policies.

Third-party providers retain and delete data according to their own policies and controls. For information processed by Google and Apple services, review the privacy documentation linked in this policy. You may also contact us using the address below for privacy-related questions or requests.

11. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. International Processing

Third-party services may process information in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your region.

13. Security

We do not operate a user account system for this app and we do not store your custom greeting text on our own servers. We use reputable service providers and limit app permissions. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and publish the revised policy at the app's privacy policy URL.
